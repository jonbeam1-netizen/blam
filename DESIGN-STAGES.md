# B.L.A.M. stage and target design (proposal, 2026-10-28)

For Jon to approve or edit. Nothing here is built yet. Every stage must: be fully seeded/deterministic,
work in versus, run on phone, have a briefing card, and work the B.L.A.M. logo into its environment.

## 1. Target and distraction catalogue

| Type | What it is | Shoot? | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paddle | bullseye disc on a post | yes | the bread and butter |
| Bomb | black skull ball with a lit fuse | NO (life -1) | tension; capped per tier |
| Flip-bomb | bomb whose fuse burns out, then flips into a paddle for a split second | yes, late | rewards watching |
| Decoy | a BOMB painted like a target: round, rings on it, but a fuse and a subtle tell (wrong ring colours, a wobble) | NO (life -1) | look before you shoot |
| Bystander | hands up, bright clothes | NO (life -1) | the Point Blank classic |
| Criminal | dark coat, hat, gun drawn | yes | pops out from behind bystanders, doors, windows |
| Fake mascot | knock-off Deputy Duck, wrong hat or badge | yes (only the fakes) | spot-the-difference |
| Swarmer | small thing that comes at the camera in numbers | yes | aliens, bats, bees |
| Protect-me | something you must keep alive by shooting what threatens it | shoot the threats | the boat and cannonballs |
| Timed cover | a shutter, door, lid or elevator that opens briefly | depends what is behind | pace and rhythm |
| Chain | a row that must be shot in order (1-2-3, colours, sizes) | in order | brain + aim |
| Moving cover | a sliding wall or crowd that hides targets as it passes | wait for the gap | patience |
| Quick-draw | one target, one shot, very short window | yes | reflex |
| Scoring board | paper silhouette with rings 100/80/60 | yes, score at least N | accuracy, limited shots |

Distractions that may layer on a stage (Jon approved only these): a bird or balloon drifting through,
and the duck heckling from the corner. NO light flicker, NO camera flash, NO falling props, NO fake HIT sign.

## 2. Next stages (proposed order)

### Stage 2: ALLEY (bystanders and criminals)
- **Theme:** a night alley behind a diner: dumpsters, a fire escape, steam from a grate, neon. Not Point Blank's park.
- **Targets:** criminals pop out from behind dumpsters, doors and the fire escape; bystanders (cook, delivery kid, old lady with a tiny dog) walk through with hands up. Cardboard-cutout feel, volumetric.
- **Rule:** shoot N criminals, don't shoot bystanders. Higher tiers: criminals and bystanders overlap and swap places, shorter windows, a criminal uses a bystander as cover.
- **Twist:** a criminal who fakes surrender (hands up, then reaches). Decide if that's fair before building.
- **Logo:** a neon "B.L.A.M. DINER" sign on the wall, half the letters buzzing.
- **Ammo:** magazine, manual reload.

### Stage 3: BONEYARD (the skeleton pile)
- **Theme:** a crypt floor heaped with bones under an arch, moonlight through a grate. Ours, not their graveyard.
- **Targets:** skeletons lurch out of the pile in random spots, some shake their skull before dropping back; a bomb skull with a candle on top hides among them; at high tiers two or three rise at once and one is a flip-bomb.
- **Rule:** shoot N skeletons in T seconds.
- **Twist:** shooting a skeleton's skull vs its ribs gives different points; a skeleton that throws its own skull at the camera.
- **Logo:** carved into the arch keystone.
- **Ammo:** unlimited at Beginner, magazine above.

### Stage 4: SKY SHOW (fireworks)
- **Theme:** a city skyline at night from a rooftop, a river reflecting the lights.
- **Targets:** rockets shoot up on arcs with a glowing head; shoot the head before it fizzles and it bursts big (points scale with height: high burst = more). NO duds (Jon: boring and mean). At high tiers a "bomb" rocket with a black skull trail must not be shot.
- **Approved 2026-10-29.** Fable's picks: hard shot cap (variants add a magazine version), finale spells B.L.A.M. in the sky on clear, rocket speed builds up from Beginner to fast at Insane, balloon drifts by, duck heckles.
- **Rule:** make N fireworks explode.
- **Twist:** chain bursts: hitting one near another sets it off for a combo.
- **Logo:** spelled out in the sky by the final burst of the stage, and on a water tower on the roof.
- **Ammo:** hard cap, so every shot counts.

### Later candidates (one line each)
- **DOCK**: protect the duck in a rowboat from cannonballs fired by a pirate fleet.
- **ELEVATORS**: a lobby of lifts, doors open on bystanders or criminals for a second each.
- **DOPPELDUCKS**: a wall of Deputy Ducks, shoot the 5 fakes (wrong badge, wrong hat, wrong eyes).
- **INVASION**: toy UFOs swarm down toward a voxel Earth, numbers grow each wave, mothership boss.
- **MOLE HOLES**: pop-up board with holes, targets and bombs peek out, whack-a-mole pacing.
- **PAPER RANGE**: scoring silhouette, 6 shots, score at least 480.
- **VAULT**: a bank vault with timed shutters sliding over targets.
- **SKEE BALL** (Jon's idea): balls roll down a skee-ball lane; the hole a ball drops into decides whether a target or a bomb pops up there. Shoot the targets as they appear.
